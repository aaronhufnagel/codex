```text
"If I can laugh, I can live." —Christopher Reeve

Reminder that today and tomorrow we are using the Testing Schedule.

Summitt (all day)--Shaterri Stanley
Taylor (1/2 day AM)--No Sub Required
Campbell--No Sub Required

Floating Subs — Please contact Karen if needed: Maribel Aguilar Salinas is floating today.

Have a wonderful day, everyone! 🌟
```

Notes:
- Uses `Homecroft family`
- Adds a brief quote connection after the greeting
- Adds an operational reminder as its own short paragraph
- Uses `Today's Absences:` heading
- Keeps the floating sub section concise
- Uses the standard closing
