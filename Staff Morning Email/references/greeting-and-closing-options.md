# Greeting And Closing Options

Use these options when drafting the morning staff email.

## Greetings

Rotate daily. Do not repeat the same greeting two days in a row. On Fridays, prefer a Friday-specific option.

- "Welcome to another wonderful [Day]."
- "Let's make it a great [Day]."
- "Here we go — it's [Day]!"
- "Ready for an amazing [Day]."
- "Let's embrace this beautiful [Day]."
- "Happy [Day]!"
- "Let's finish the week strong!" (Fridays)
